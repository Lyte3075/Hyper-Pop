How to Play Gladihoppers and Improve

Start with the controls before worrying about score or speed. The catalog lists: WASD Keys: Move; Arrow Keys: Attack; Space: Switch Stance; Esc: Pause. Test the main inputs in a low-pressure attempt, note how quickly the game reacts, and then build a repeatable rhythm instead of pressing more buttons than the situation requires.

Keep part of your attention on the next hazard rather than only the current target. Looking one step ahead usually reduces panic inputs and makes recovery more predictable. Once the controls feel natural, improvement comes from cleaner positioning, earlier reads and fewer desperate inputs.

A useful review habit is to ask why an attempt ended rather than simply restarting. One clear observation—late timing, poor positioning, a rushed decision or an unnecessary correction—gives the next attempt a specific purpose and makes progress easier to repeat.
